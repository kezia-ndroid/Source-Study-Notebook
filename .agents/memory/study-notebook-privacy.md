---
name: Study notebook privacy boundary
description: Privacy and source-grounding constraints for future study notebook changes.
---

Keep course material and approved excerpts in browser storage by default. Do not send source text to an external model or service unless the user explicitly opts in after a clear disclosure. Keep each study item linked to a verbatim source excerpt, separate source text from learner interpretation, and require approval before an item enters the library.

**Why:** The notebook is intended to be private, and its study materials must remain traceable to the user's source rather than present unsupported claims as facts.

**How to apply:** Prefer local processing and persistence. If cloud sync or AI assistance is added, make the data flow opt-in and visible, preserve exact evidence links, and keep generated content separate from source facts.
